Stephanie Snider and Stepan Dubrovskiy are professional figure skating coaches at Sharks Ice in San Jose, California. Stephanie is originally from Denmark and Stepan is from Russia. They are a former junior level ice dance team representing Denmark internationally on the Grand Prix Circuit and Junior Worlds.

Stephanie and Stepan formed their partnership and began training in June 2011. Stepan was a former pair skater, but decided to relocate from Moscow, Russia to San Jose, California to skate with Stephanie and train under ice dance coaches Sergei Ponomarenko and Marina Klimova, the 1992 Olympic Gold Medalists.

From 2012-2013, they skated professionally with Royal Caribbean Cruise Lines, traveling to over 20 different countries.

Following a successful tour with Royal Caribbean, Stephanie and Stepan moved to Stepan’s home town, Krasnoyarsk, Russia, and opened up their own figure skating program. They coached competitive figure skaters and beginners at Rassvet Skating School. Their program included group instruction, private lessons, off-ice conditioning, and choreography. They were invited to several Siberian cities for camps and competitions.

In April 2014, Stephanie and Stepan were married in a small ceremony in Krasnoyarsk, Russia. Following their wedding, they celebrated their honeymoon in Phuket, Thailand.

The couple moved back to San Jose, California in August 2015, and are teaching figure skating at Sharks Ice at San Jose. They teach skaters of all levels and ages and are currently accepting new students.
